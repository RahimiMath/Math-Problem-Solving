## Problem 1 - Multiples of 3 or 5

### Problem:
If we list all the natural numbers below 10 that are multiples of 3 or 5, we get:
3, 5, 6, 9

The sum of these numbers is 23.

Find the sum of all the multiples of 3 or 5 below 1000.

---

### Thinking Process:

1. We are looking for numbers divisible by 3 or 5
2. Instead of checking one by one, we use a mathematical pattern
3. Sum multiples of 3 + sum multiples of 5
4. Subtract multiples of 15 (to avoid duplication)

---

### Final Answer:
233168
